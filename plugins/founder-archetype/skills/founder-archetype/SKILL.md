## The 5 Archetypes

**The Initiatrix**
Starts movements. Activates others. Sees what's possible before it exists. Leads with vision and urgency. Often the first in their space to say the thing everyone was thinking.

**The Weaver**
Connects threads, people, and ideas that others don't see as related. Builds ecosystems. Creates through relationship and orchestration. The one who makes the whole greater than its parts.

**The Oracle**
Transmits knowing. Sees what others miss. Speaks truth before it's popular. Leads with insight and depth. Clients don't just buy their services — they buy their perspective.

**The Architect**
Designs systems and structures. Brings order to complexity. Builds things that last. Leads with precision and clarity. Clients feel safe because everything is considered.

**The Mirror**
Witnesses transformation. Reflects truth back to others. Holds space for becoming. Leads with presence and depth of seeing. Clients discover themselves through working with them.
